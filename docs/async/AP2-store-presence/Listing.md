## Long description (1,026 / 4,000 characters)

Whopper is a burger on the run. Swipe through a busy kitchen, dodge whatever’s coming and see how far you get before you’re burnt.

SWIPE TO SURVIVE

- Swipe left or right to change lane, even mid-air
- Swipe up to jump over knives
- Swipe down to slide under dish racks, or slam down from a jump
- Swipes register the moment your finger moves, so there’s no waiting to let go

A KITCHEN THAT FIGHTS BACK

- 10 hazard patterns, from bottle walls to knife-and-bottle combos and staircases
- The kitchen speeds up the longer you last
- Survive two minutes and the order’s up!

NEW MISSIONS EVERY RUN

- 3 random missions each run: run a distance, grab pickups, jump, slide or switch lanes
- Finish one and the bonus coins are yours straight away

EARN AND UPGRADE

- Collect pickups to earn coins
- Spend coins between runs to make every pickup worth more

PLAY IT YOUR WAY

- Pause any time; your run waits for you
- High FPS mode for 120 Hz screens
- No internet or account needed, and no ads
- Your progress is saved on your phone
