## Long description (786 / 4,000 characters)

Whopper is a burger on the run. Swipe through a busy kitchen and dodge the knives, bottles and dish racks coming your way. How far can you get before you’re burnt?

- Swipe left or right to change lane, even in mid-air
- Swipe up to jump over knives, or down to slide under dish racks and slam down from a jump
- Swipes register as soon as your finger moves, so the controls feel instant
- 10 hazard patterns, and the kitchen speeds up the longer you last
- 3 new missions every run, each paying bonus coins when you finish it
- Collect pickups and spend your coins between runs to make every pickup worth more
- Survive two minutes and the order’s up
- Pause any time, and switch on High FPS mode for 120 Hz screens

No account and no ads; your progress is saved locally on your phone.
